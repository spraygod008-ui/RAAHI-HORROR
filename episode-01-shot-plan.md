# RAAHI HORROR — Episode 01 Production Shot Plan
## आख़िरी बस का रहस्यमयी यात्री
Target runtime: 15–20 minutes | Format: 2D animated horror | Language: Hindi

## Production approach
Build the episode as layered 2D animation: character layers, background plates, foreground fog/rain, and camera moves. Use limited animation for dialogue and suspense, reserving full motion for key scares and the final reveal. Maintain the Character Bible exactly.

## Sequence plan
| Sequence | Approx. time | Visual action | Camera / animation | Sound |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| 1. Cold open | 0:00–1:15 | Empty midnight road; distant red bus emerges through fog. | Wide establishing shot; slow push-in; headlights bloom through layered fog. | Wind, distant engine, low drone. |
| 2. Arjun arrives | 1:15–3:00 | Arjun waits alone and checks the time. | Medium shot; insert of phone/clock; subtle jacket and hair movement. | Night insects, phone vibration, road ambience. |
| 3. The bus stops | 3:00–4:30 | Old bus brakes beside him; door opens slowly. | Low angle on wheels, then door; restrained camera shake. | Brake hiss, metal door creak, engine idle. |
| 4. Inside the bus | 4:30–6:30 | Arjun notices silent passengers and the conductor. | Slow aisle tracking shot; reaction close-ups; parallax seats. | Engine rumble, seat creaks, ticket machine click. |
| 5. Meera's warning | 6:30–8:30 | Meera quietly warns Arjun not to get off at the next stop. | Shot/reverse-shot; hold on Meera's eyes; minimal lip animation. | Dialogue close, music drops to near silence. |
| 6. The closed road | 8:30–10:30 | Road sign reveals the route was closed 20 years ago. | Insert on sign through window; slow rack-focus effect. | Wind rises, faint whisper, bass pulse. |
| 7. The accident memory | 10:30–13:00 | Fragments suggest a past bus accident. | Short memory flashes, silhouettes, broken glass reflections; no graphic gore. | Distorted impact, muffled ringing, reversed texture. |
| 8. The final ticket | 13:00–15:30 | Conductor issues Arjun a ticket that changes the meaning of the journey. | Extreme close-up of ticket; slow push to Arjun's face. | Ticket tear, clock tick, heartbeat. |
| 9. The reveal | 15:30–18:00 | Arjun realizes he is tied to the old accident and the endless route. | Controlled close-ups; reveal through reflection; final wide shot of bus disappearing. | Music swell, engine recedes, final silence. |

## Shot construction rules
1. Every shot must have a clear focal point and readable silhouette.
2. Use 2–3 depth layers for parallax: foreground, character/midground, background.
3. Keep dialogue shots mostly still; animate eyes, mouth, breathing, and small gestures.
4. Use slow camera movement for dread; avoid random zooms and excessive shake.
5. Keep scares readable: establish the space before the reveal.
6. Do not introduce new characters or story facts not present in the screenplay without approval.

## Asset list to create next
- Arjun: front, 3/4, side, back, and six expressions.
- Meera: front, 3/4, side, back, and four expressions.
- Conductor: front, 3/4, side, back, and three expressions.
- Bus exterior: front, side, rear; bus interior: aisle and seat rows.
- Backgrounds: lonely highway, bus stop, bus interior, closed-road sign, accident-memory road.
- Effects: fog layers, light beams, rain overlay, window reflections, subtle flicker.

## Episode assembly order
1. Lock character reference sheets.
2. Create reusable backgrounds and bus layouts.
3. Create scene panels using the approved screenplay.
4. Animate dialogue and key actions.
5. Add ambience, Foley, score, and Hindi voice tracks.
6. Export a low-resolution animatic for timing review before final rendering.
