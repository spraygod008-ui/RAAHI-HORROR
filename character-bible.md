# RAAHI HORROR — Character Bible
## Episode 01: आख़िरी बस का रहस्यमयी यात्री

This document is the visual continuity reference for every scene, thumbnail, poster, and future episode asset.

## Global art direction
- Format: original 2D animated horror.
- Visual language: cinematic anime-inspired illustration, clean readable silhouettes, detailed backgrounds, controlled shadows.
- Palette: midnight blue, charcoal, cold moonlight, muted amber practical lights, restrained warning red.
- Continuity rule: keep each character's face, hair, clothing, body proportions, and signature props identical in every shot. Do not redesign between scenes.

## 1. Arjun — protagonist
- Age: 25.
- Build: slim/average, youthful.
- Hair: thick, messy black hair; same silhouette and length in every scene.
- Face: expressive dark eyes, clean-shaven, youthful features.
- Outfit: brown casual jacket over a plain black T-shirt, dark trousers, black backpack.
- Props: backpack; phone when the screenplay calls for it; flashlight only if specified by the shot.
- Personality in acting: observant and curious at first; increasingly tense; fear should feel restrained and believable.
- Fixed color keys: brown jacket, black shirt, dark backpack.
- Do not add facial hair, change jacket color, or give him a different hairstyle.

## 2. Meera — mysterious passenger
- Age: approximately 24.
- Build: slim.
- Hair: long, dark hair; natural, slightly loose.
- Outfit: simple pale/white traditional outfit with a muted blue dupatta.
- Face/acting: quiet, watchful, melancholy; avoid exaggerated monster expressions in her early scenes.
- Story function: the mysterious female passenger. She is not a separate character from the ghostly woman shown in promotional concept art.
- Keep her appearance subtle and consistent; supernatural effects are lighting and acting cues, not a costume redesign.

## 3. Conductor — old ticket collector
- Age: around 60.
- Build: lean, slightly stooped.
- Face: deep-set eyes, lined face, grey moustache.
- Outfit: old, faded khaki bus-conductor uniform and matching cap.
- Props: old ticket machine and paper tickets.
- Acting: unnervingly calm, economical movements, long pauses; never play him as a loud cartoon villain.

## 4. Driver
- Mostly kept in shadow or seen in partial silhouette.
- Dark, worn driver uniform.
- Face should remain hidden until the screenplay explicitly reveals it.
- Movement: minimal; hands on steering wheel, silhouette against dashboard light.

## 5. Bus — recurring story object
- Old, worn red public bus with faded paint, dusty windows, and warm amber headlights.
- Interior: dim amber ceiling lights, worn blue/grey seats, scratched metal poles.
- Keep bus shape, headlight placement, door, and interior layout consistent across all shots.
- Avoid changing the bus into a modern coach.

## Expression and pose library
For Arjun: neutral/alert, suspicious glance, confused, startled, controlled fear, exhausted shock.
For Meera: neutral, distant gaze, quiet warning, sorrowful, unsettling stillness.
For Conductor: neutral, faint unsettling smile, stern, eyes lowered.
For Driver: silhouette only.

## Production continuity checklist
- [ ] Arjun: brown jacket + black shirt + messy black hair + backpack.
- [ ] Meera: pale outfit + muted blue dupatta + long dark hair.
- [ ] Conductor: faded khaki uniform + cap + ticket machine.
- [ ] Driver's face stays obscured unless scripted.
- [ ] Same bus design and interior layout in every scene.
- [ ] Lighting stays within midnight blue / charcoal / muted amber palette.
