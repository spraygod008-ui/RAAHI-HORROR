RAAHI HORROR — EPISODE 01
Title: Aakhri Bus Ka Rahasyamayi Yatri
Format: Original 2D animated horror
Target duration: 15–20 minutes
Language: Hindi

MAIN CHARACTER
Arjun, 25 years old. Brown jacket, black hair. He boards the last bus just before midnight.

STORY PREMISE
At midnight, Arjun boards an almost empty bus. The passengers sit silently. An old conductor refuses to answer simple questions. The bus takes a road that was closed twenty years ago. A mysterious woman passenger seems to know Arjun, although he has never met her.

EPISODE STRUCTURE
00:00–01:00 — Cold open: the bus appears through dense fog; a passenger whispers, “Yeh bus wapas nahi jaati.”
01:00–03:00 — Arjun reaches the lonely bus stop and boards.
03:00–07:00 — The silent passengers, flickering lights, and strange conductor build tension.
07:00–10:00 — Arjun notices that nobody has a reflection in the window.
10:00–14:00 — The mysterious woman tells him the road has been closed for twenty years.
14:00–18:00 — Arjun discovers the bus passengers are connected to an old accident.
18:00–20:00 — Twist: the conductor calls Arjun by the name on an old passenger list dated twenty years earlier.

PRODUCTION NOTES
Visual style: cinematic 2D animated horror, dark blue night palette, fog, controlled camera moves, expressive character poses.
Sound: low drone, distant engine, rain, metal rattles, restrained impact sounds. Use original or properly licensed audio.
